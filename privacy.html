Privacy Policy for CareerCity

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CareerCity, developed and published by Kaika Games Studio, handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

CareerCity does not require users to create an online account and does not collect personal information such as email addresses, phone numbers, real names, precise location, contacts, or payment information.

At the beginning of the game, the player may choose a player name. This name is used only within the game and is stored locally on the user's device as part of the game save data. It is not transmitted to Kaika Games Studio or to any external server.

Game Save Data

CareerCity stores game progress and settings locally on the user's device. Kaika Games Studio does not receive, access, or store this local game data.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of CareerCity does not contain advertising, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Sharing of Information

Because CareerCity does not collect personal or sensitive user information, Kaika Games Studio does not sell, rent, or share such information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

CareerCity does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. No online account is required, and gameplay data remains stored locally on the user's device.

Data Security

Since CareerCity does not transmit personal information to Kaika Games Studio, personal user data is not stored on our servers. Users are responsible for the security of the device on which their local save data is stored.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if CareerCity's features change, including if future versions add advertising, analytics, cloud services, online accounts, or other features that affect how user information is handled. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
